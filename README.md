🛍️ Vrinda Store Sales Analysis Dashboard
Project Objective
Vrinda Store wants to create an annual sales report to understand customer behavior and identify opportunities to increase sales.

Tools Used
Microsoft Excel
Pivot Tables
Pivot Charts
Slicers
Dashboard Design
Dashboard Preview

Business Questions
Compare sales and orders using a single chart.
Which month achieved the highest sales and orders?
Who purchased more: men or women?
Which states contribute the most sales?
Which sales channel contributes the highest revenue?
Which product category performs best?
Key Insights
Women contribute approximately 65% of sales.
Adults aged 30–49 years generate around 50% of total orders.
Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are the top-performing states.
Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra contribute nearly 80% of sales.
Final Recommendation
Target women customers aged 30–49 years in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh through Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra using targeted offers and promotional campaigns.
