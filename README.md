# **🛍️ Vrinda Store Sales Analysis Dashboard**

## **Project Objective**

##### Vrinda Store wants to create an Annual Sales Report for 2024 to understand customer behavior and identify opportunities to increase sales in 2025.



## Business Questions

* ##### Compare Sales and Orders using a single chart.
* ##### Which month got the highest Sales and Orders?
* ##### Who purchased more, Men or Women?
* ##### What are the different Order Status categories?
* ##### List the Top 10 States contributing to sales.
* ##### Analyze the relationship between Age Group and Gender based on the number of orders.
* ##### Which sales channel contributes the maximum sales?
* ##### Which product category has the highest sales?

## Key Insights

* ##### Women are more likely to buy compared to men (\~65%).
* ##### Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh are the top 3 states contributing approximately 35% of total sales.
* ##### Adult age group (30–49 years) contributes around 50% of total orders.
* ##### Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra contribute approximately 80% of total sales.

## Final Recommendation

##### To improve sales in 2025, Vrinda Store should focus on:

##### 

* ##### Targeting women customers.
* ##### Focusing on the 30–49 years age group.
* ##### Running promotional campaigns in Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh.
* ##### Leveraging Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra channels.
* ##### Offering attractive discounts, coupons, and personalized promotions.

## Project Outcome

##### This dashboard helps identify customer behavior patterns, top-performing sales channels, leading states, and growth opportunities, enabling data-driven business decisions.
